# Academic Research Data Request Specification: Delhi Traffic Police

**Target Organization**: Delhi Traffic Police / Government of NCT of Delhi  
**Status**: `DRAFT - REQUEST_READY`

---

## 1. Research Scope
Academic data request for micro-level road crash logs and verified high-fatality blackspot spatial records across Delhi NCT for research evaluation in SafeRoute AI.

---

## 2. Requested Fields
- **Location**: Exact GPS coordinates or nearest landmark street junction.
- **Timestamp**: Date and hour of crash occurrence.
- **Severity**: Fatalities, Serious Injuries, Minor Injuries.
- **Blackspot Prior Data**: Official blackspot centroids, 3-year fatality thresholds, and road agency classifications.

---

## 3. Privacy & Usage Guarantee
- Non-commercial academic research use only.
- Strict PII exclusion.
- Full credit and attribution to Delhi Traffic Police in research papers and technical reports.
